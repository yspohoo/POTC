Get Amazon dataset from:
https://jmcauley.ucsd.edu/data/amazon/index_2014.html

Dataset used in POTC:
Book domain dataset: reviews_Books_5.json (8.9GB);
Movie domain dataset: reviews_Movies_and_TV_5.json (1.9GB);
Music domain dataset: reviews_CDs_and_Vinyl_5.json (1.3GB);
Details can be seen in ./amazon/readme.txt.
